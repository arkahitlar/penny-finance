A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

Your data, explained.

Penny is a spending journal. It does not connect to your bank or move money.

When you sign in

Google confirms your identity. Penny stores your Google account identifier, name, and email address to keep your journal separate from other accounts. Penny does not receive your Google password, and it does not request access to Gmail, Drive, or your contacts.

When you add an expense

Your description is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to identify the purchase, amount, category, and possible discretionary spending. A private preview is stored in the app’s Turso database for review and expires after 15 minutes. Expired previews are removed during later preview requests. The expense is added to your journal only after you confirm its category, expense date, and Cash or Credit card. This stores only the payment type, never a card number. The original description is not saved there; a hash of your confirmation choices is retained to prevent duplicate submissions. Avoid including account numbers or other sensitive details in a description.

Your session and reports

A browser cookie keeps you signed in. The cookie is only accessible to the server and expires after 30 days; signing out revokes it. Expenses and reports are available only to your signed-in account. Downloaded CSV reports are files on your device, so keep them somewhere appropriate.

Sample journal

The sample journal is separate from your account. Its example data and any entries you add stay in memory in that browser tab. It does not send those descriptions to an AI provider or save them in the database.

Storage and the app owner

You can edit or delete your own saved expenses from Today and Reports. The expense date controls where an entry appears; its original recording time is retained separately. Deleting an expense removes its item and amount from the active journal and reports. Minimal submission identifiers remain to prevent a retried save from recreating it. Provider backups may retain earlier database versions under their retention policies. The owner of this deployment administers its database and hosting. Contact the person who provided your Penny link for questions about your stored data or to request its removal. This page describes how this version of the application handles data.
